Important visitor information

Pre-booked timed entry is required. Crop availability changes daily and cannot be guaranteed. There are no toilet facilities on the farm; facilities are available inside the Garden Centre but are not within walking distance. Pet dogs are not permitted, except assistance dogs.

Growing since 1871

From Borough Market growers to a destination for generations.

Garsons began in Esher as a family-run market-gardening business supplying fresh produce to London’s wholesale markets, especially Borough Market. The farm shop and first garden centre followed, and Garsons Titchfield later joined the family.

Today the business remains family-run, bringing together horticulture, food, leisure, community and practical gardening knowledge across two award-winning locations.
